FAQ

Questions

What happens when the free trial ends?

Blocking turns off and the timer and music stop. The popup shows a button to buy a license. Nothing is charged automatically, because you never enter a card for the trial.

How do I unlock it after buying?

Your license key arrives by email right after payment. Open the BlockTheNoise popup, click Unlock, paste the key and press Activate.

Who processes my payment?

Payments are handled by Dodo Payments, our reseller and merchant of record. Your card or UPI details go to them, never to us or to the extension.

Does BlockTheNoise track what I do?

No. There are no analytics and no tracking. Your settings stay in your browser. The only thing sent anywhere is your license key, to check it with Dodo Payments. Read the privacy policy.

Which browsers does it work in?

Google Chrome, plus browsers built on Chrome that can install from the Chrome Web Store, such as Microsoft Edge and Brave.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If BlockTheNoise isn't right for you, ask within 7 days of buying for a full refund. See the refund policy.
