## Description

Your attention is your most valuable resource. BlockTheNoise protects it.

BlockTheNoise brings three focus tools together in one toolbar button: a site blocker, a Pomodoro timer and focus music. Start a session, and the distractions go quiet while the music keeps you in flow.

✨ TRY EVERYTHING FREE FOR 14 DAYS
Every feature is unlocked for 14 days, with no card and no sign-up. If it helps you focus, keep it forever with a one-time payment of ₹800. No subscription.

🚫 BLOCK THE DISTRACTIONS
• Instagram, YouTube and Facebook are blocked from the start
• Add any other site in one click, such as Twitter, Reddit or news sites
• Block all the time, or only while a focus session is running
• Blocked sites show a calm reminder with your remaining focus time instead of the feed
• Tabs that are already open get caught too

⏱️ POMODORO TIMER
• 25-minute focus sessions with 5-minute breaks, and a longer break after every 4 sessions
• Set the length of every phase yourself
• See the minutes left right on the toolbar icon
• Get a notification when each focus session or break ends
• Start the next phase automatically, or start each one yourself

🎧 MUSIC FOR CREATIVE FLOW
• Built-in ambient, downtempo and instrumental hip-hop radio stations
• Brown and pink noise that is generated on your computer and works offline
• Add your own audio stream links
• Music can start when a focus session begins and pause on breaks

🔒 PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account and no sign-up
• No tracking, no analytics and no ads
• Your settings stay in your browser. Only your license key is checked with our payment provider.

How to use it:
1. Click the BlockTheNoise icon in your toolbar.
2. Press Start to begin a focus session.
3. Get to work. BlockTheNoise handles the rest.
4. Bought a license? Open the popup, click Unlock and paste the key from your purchase email.

Radio stations are provided by SomaFM (somafm.com), listener-supported internet radio.
